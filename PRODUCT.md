# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, demo first:

- **Readers and judges of the DEV Hacktoberfest 2026 challenge** (week 4, *Ghost in the Machine*), meeting Kuyō through a GIF and a published cemetery link inside a post. They decide in seconds whether it is memorable.
- **Developers who run `kuyo bury` on their own repository**, then open the generated cemetery to see what they deleted and how. Repos range from a handful of souls to a few hundred (one real repo produced 137).

## Product Purpose

Kuyō reads a git repository's history, finds every deleted file and holds a funeral for each: how it lived, how it died, how restless its soul is. Java computes the facts deterministically; a local Gemma model on Ollama writes the epitaphs. The output is one self-contained `cemiterio.html`.

Success: a viewer feels the code they deleted was honored, smiles at an epitaph, and understands at a glance which deaths were violent.

## Positioning

The code never leaves the machine. Offline, free per run, model swappable by configuration. The facts are Java's, the words are Gemma's.

## Operating Context

- Generated by a Quarkus CLI through a Qute template; opened locally in a browser or published as a static page.
- Kuyō is a Japanese memorial rite for objects that served well. Finados (2 November in Brazil) is the cultural echo for the author.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single self-contained HTML file: CSS, SVG and JS inline. No downloaded images; every visual hand-drawn in SVG/CSS. No external fonts or scripts.
- Data per soul: path, file name, cause of death (violenta, prematura, esquecida, natural), restlessness 0–1 with five factor scores, birth and death dates, hour of death, last words (commit message first line), death commit hash, authors, epitaph (may be absent with `--sem-epitafios`).
- Restlessness bands drive the haunting: < 0.3 one blue flame; 0.3–0.6 two flames; ≥ 0.6 three violet flames, tremor, yūrei on hover.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all animation.
- Epitaphs in English by default, Portuguese with `--lang pt`.

## Brand Commitments

- Tone: solemn with dry humor. Respectful of the kuyō tradition.
- Must not read as generic Halloween (pumpkins, bats, Christian crosses, blood) nor as cute or cartoonish.
- Name: Kuyō 供養. Hand-drawn banner exists at `docs/banner.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real epitaphs and scan output from the author's repositories (brasa, octane, kakeizu, stocklentes-hub). No testimonials, users or metrics exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Honor before spectacle: every effect serves the memorial.
2. Facts are computed, never invented; the page shows them truthfully.
3. Restlessness must be legible at a glance.
4. Everything stays local and self-contained.
